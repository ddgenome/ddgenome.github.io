By adopting DevOps, developers are owning more of the software development and
delivery flow.  Societal trends over the last few millenia has been from less
specialization to more specialization.  Hunters and gatherers.  Agriculture.
Does the DevOps trend buck this trend?  Is developing and delivering software
fundamentally different than other jobs?
